ComLab PC Reservation System

Section 2: Problem Statement & User Goal

Group 3

The Problem

Students frequently face uncertainty and wasted time checking computer lab availability manually. This leads to laboratory congestion, overlapping session attempts, and inefficient resource allocation during peak academic hours.

The User Goal

Provide students with a seamless, real-time web portal to check computer laboratory status, reserve available workstations instantly, and receive a verified entry pass—eliminating wait times and scheduling conflicts.
